Kari Irwin Otteburn is a senior researcher at the Leuven Centre for Global Governance Studies of KU Leuven in Belgium. Her work is situated within the broader field of international political economy and explores the intersections of global economic governance and global sustainability governance, contributing to scholarship on business and human rights, transnational governance and private regulation. Her research emphasizes promoting human rights and environmental sustainability and mitigating the negative impacts of trade, investment and transnational business activities, with particular focus on corporate accountability. Geographically, she focuses on the role of the European Union in global governance, especially through the Union's interactions with the United States and (South) Asia.

Kari earned her PhD in political science from KU Leuven in 2024, following a master’s degree in European Studies from KU Leuven in 2018 and a bachelor’s degree from Illinois Wesleyan University (United States) in 2009. Her doctoral dissertation examined access to remedy for human rights abuses linked to transnational business operations.

Her scholarly contributions have been featured in leading journals, including Global Policy, The International Journal of Human Rights, The Journal of World Investment and Trade, Asia Europe Journal, International Review of Public Policy, as well as in numerous edited volumes. She is also a co-editor of  the Europe-Asia Connectivities in Geopolitical Times (Palgrave Macmillan, forthcoming 2027) and the Research Handbook on Global Governance, Business and Human Rights (Edward Elgar, 2022).

She is teaching Global Environmental Politics for the Masters of European Studies and the Master of International Public Policy at KU Leuven in the academic year 2025-2026. In addition, she regularly lectures on European Union institutions and policymaking, the EU in global governance, the EU as a human rights actor, business and human rights and EU trade and investment policy. She supervises masters' students of international law in legal clinics on business and human rights and corporate accountability topics. 

Originally from the Chicago area, Kari is currently based in Brussels, Belgium. She speaks English (native), French, Hindi and Urdu. She is currently learning Dutch.
